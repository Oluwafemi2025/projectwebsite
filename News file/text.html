CMUL Alumni Class of 98 Empowers Students with Research Grants

The College of Medicine, University of Lagos (CMUL) Alumni Class of 1998 has demonstrated its commitment to supporting the next generation of medical professionals with the presentation of research grants to students of the College. At a brief ceremony held on Wednesday, August 27, 2025 at the New Board Room of the College, ten (10) 500-level MBBS students received a cash award of One hundred thousand naira (₦100,000) each to support their research projects.
